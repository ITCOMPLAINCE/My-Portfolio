About me

Lagos, Nigeria — writing since 2021. Self-published across personal finance, discipline and mindset.

I write for people who are tired of long books that say very little.

My name is Afolabi Ayelotan. I turn hard-earned lessons on money, focus and self-discipline into short, direct ebooks — the kind you can finish in one sitting and actually act on.

I didn't start out as a writer. I started out broke, distracted, and looking for answers that weren't wrapped in 300 pages of filler. So I write the books I wish someone had handed me — plain language, real steps, nothing padded out to look impressive.

Every title on this page is self-published, tested on real readers before release, and built to be read on a phone during a commute, not shelved and forgotten.
